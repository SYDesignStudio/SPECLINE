Privacy notice

How we handle personal data

This notice covers the Specline website and the Specline service. It is written to be read, not to be got past.

Version 1.0 · 6 September 2026

1Who we are

SY Design Studio Ltd, of 49 Durham Avenue, Hounslow TW5 0HG, trading as Specline, is the data controller for the personal data described in sections 3 and 4. You can reach us at info@specline.co.uk about anything in this notice, including to exercise any of the rights in section 8.

2Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies. There is no analytics, no advertising pixel and no third-party tracking on it. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google and Google will see your IP address as part of serving the font file.

The Specline application stores your practice settings and unsent work in your own browser so that the app works. That storage stays on your device and is not tracking.

5Your clients' personal data

A job record can contain personal data about someone who is not our customer, most obviously a client's name and a site address. For that data you are the controller and we act as your processor. On your instructions we will:

  • process it only to provide the service to you, and not for our own purposes;
  • keep it confidential and require the same of anyone we use to help run the service;
  • apply appropriate security, as described in section 7;
  • not engage another processor without telling you first and giving you a chance to object;
  • help you respond to a request from an individual, and to a personal data breach;
  • delete or return it at the end of the contract, as section 4 sets out; and
  • make available what you reasonably need to show we are meeting these obligations.

These commitments are the processing terms referred to in our terms of supply.

6Who else is involved

We keep the number of third parties small and will name them here rather than describe them vaguely.

  • Hostinger hosts this website and the waiting list, in the EU.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on these pages, as described in section 2.
  • Anthropic hosts the Specline application in its current form, so job records saved in the app are stored on its infrastructure.
  • A payment processor will be added when subscriptions open. We will name it here before we take a payment, and we will never hold your full card details ourselves.

Where a provider processes data outside the UK, we rely on the UK's adequacy regulations or on the International Data Transfer Addendum to the standard contractual clauses.

We will also disclose data where the law requires it. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it to train machine learning models.

7Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to the waiting list and to subscriber data is limited to those who need it. The waiting list is stored outside the public web directory so that it cannot be fetched from the internet. If a breach affects your rights we will tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office within the time the law requires.

8Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or provide it in a portable form. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time, and doing so does not affect what we did before. Ask us at info@specline.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, by post at Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow SK9 5AF, or on 0303 123 1113. We would rather you came to us first.

9Changes

If we change this notice we will update the version and date at the top. Where a change materially affects you we will tell you directly rather than expecting you to notice.

A note on how this notice was prepared Version 1.0 was drafted in-house and has not yet been reviewed by a solicitor or a data protection adviser. That review is scheduled before the first paid subscription is taken. It describes what we actually do today; if you find something here that does not match your experience of the service, please tell us.

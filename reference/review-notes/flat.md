# Flat Conversion (`flat`) — specification review and fix

Reviewer role: senior Architectural Technologist / building control plan-checker. File reviewed: `/tmp/review/specdata_core.FLAT.js`, `flat:` segment only (14 build-ups, 43 notes before). Fix script: `/tmp/review/fix_flat_review.py` (run; JS re-evaluates cleanly with `node`; extension and loft segments byte-identical before and after).

## (i) Verdict

Before the fix: **conditionally approvable, with requests for clarification.** The technical core (Part E 43/43/64 dB build-ups, compartmentation periods, protected common stair, Regulation 6 applicability, Q1/S2) was sound, but a plan-checker would have queried the Part G hot water clause (wrong), the deferred alarm grade, the 8000 mm² vs 10,000 mm² background-ventilator contradiction, the lighting 75%-of-fittings rule (withdrawn in AD L 2021), the Robust Details paragraph contradicting the "not available for conversions" paragraph, and the C2 applicability statement. Duplication (staircases ×2, heating ×2, ventilation ×2, testing ×2, fire periods ×2, flat entrance door FD30S ×6, 48 °C TMV ×3, Reg 16 ×2, CDM ×2) made the document long and inconsistent.

After the fix: **would approve.** Every figure checked against FACTS.md and the two sources fetched; each subject stated once; house standards applied.

## (ii) Findings and fixes

1. **Part G3 hot water clause wrong (Water & Sanitary).** Before: "Water discharged from a hot water storage system to taps and outlets is to be limited to a maximum of 60°C by an in-line thermostatic blending or tempering valve." After: cylinder capable of being stored at ≥60 °C (legionella), thermostat + non-self-resetting energy cut-out (<100 °C), unvented to BS EN 12897 by a competent-person-scheme installer and notified; bath supply ≤48 °C by TMV to BS EN 1111/1287, cited as G3(4) which Regulation 6(1)(h) applies to a house-to-flats conversion. The 48 °C TMV, previously repeated in three notes, is now stated once here.
2. **G2 figure deferred though known.** Before: "not to exceed the applicable limit … NOTE — Confirm the water efficiency limit." After: 125 l/person/day (Regulation 36) stated; NOTE retained only for the optional 110 l where a planning condition imposes it.
3. **Lighting efficacy rule out of date (Heating/Lighting note).** Before: "75 lumens per circuit watt for at least three quarters of the fittings." After: 75 lm/circuit-watt for all new fixed lighting, fittings under 5 circuit-watts excluded (AD L Vol 1 2021 Section 6).
4. **Alarm grade deferred then contradicted.** "Fire Detection and Alarm" said "grade and category to be determined by the escape strategy and confirmed with Building Control" while "Common Stair…" stated Grade D1 LD2 and the Flat Entrance Doors note gave siting rules. Merged into one rewritten "Fire Detection and Alarm" note: Grade D1 Category LD2 to BS 5839-6 (stated as exceeding the AD B Vol 1 minimum of D2 LD3, paragraphs 1.1/1.10, verified), heat alarm in kitchen, studio-flat arrangement, CO alarm to BS EN 50291 1–3 m from appliance, siting (300 mm / 7.5 m / 3 m), supply circuit, common-area BS 5839-1 not sounding in flats, single certificate statement. Alarm paragraph removed from the Common Stair note and the alarm sentence from the Flat Entrance Doors detail note; CO alarm sentence removed from the heating note.
5. **Background ventilator contradiction (Windows vs Ventilation).** Windows note required 8000 mm² per habitable room; both ventilation notes said 10,000 mm² for a flat (AD F Table 1.7). Windows note now says 10,000 mm² for a single-storey flat (8000 mm² in a maisonette), cross-referring to the Ventilation note.
6. **Ventilation stated twice.** "Background and Extract Ventilation" + "Ventilation — AD F Figures…" (System 3/4 paragraph, purge, 1.5 m flex duct, 15-minute overrun and commissioning all repeated) merged into one "Ventilation — Approved Document F" note keeping the flat-specific clauses (F1 applies to the building as converted; ducts not to pass through another flat or the common stair without fire-resisting enclosure/dampers; unit accessible within the flat; bedroom-count rates 19/25/31/37/43 l/s) and a genuine designer prompt (check purge areas against the window schedule).
7. **Robust Details contradiction (Part E).** "Pre-Completion Testing" correctly said Robust Details are not available for conversions; the appended "Part E Testing and Robust Details Evidence" note then described the Robust Details route. Merged into "Pre-Completion Sound Testing": Regulation 41, timing (before soft coverings), UKAS/ANC tester, one set per ten dwellings per construction type, failed-test/retest procedure, then the "no Robust Details" paragraph and the programme NOTE. Robust Details paragraph dropped.
8. **Fire resistance periods stated twice.** "Compartmentation" and "Minimum Periods of Fire Resistance" merged into "Compartmentation and Periods of Fire Resistance": REI 30 where top storey ≤5 m, REI 60 for >5 m to 18 m, 60 minutes always for the wall to the neighbouring building, height measured from lowest adjoining ground, 60-minute ceiling construction, full-height compartment walls, fire stopping, and one NOTE to confirm from Table B4. Consistent with FACTS.
9. **Flat entrance door FD30S stated six times** (Staircases, Means of Escape, Periods, Common Stair, Windows, Detail note). Now: full specification in "Flat Entrance Doors and Escape Within Flats — Detail", a one-line fact in the periods note and the common stair note, cross-references elsewhere. Means of Escape now says "FD30S self-closing doorset … as the Flat Entrance Doors note" and "FD20 fire doors" onto a protected entrance hall.
10. **Staircases ×2.** "Staircases and Internal Doors" + "Staircases — Detail" merged into "Staircases, Guarding and Internal Doors": full AD K private-stair paragraph (with 0.36 kN/m guarding load), the flat-specific common-stair paragraph (retain-or-rebuild, 900/1100 guarding), enclosed-stair-as-separating-wall paragraph, FD20 (E 20) internal doors.
11. **Heating ×2.** "Heating, Hot Water and Lighting Within Each Flat" + "Heating Systems — Boilers, Heat Pumps…" merged: flat-specific opening paragraph (independent systems or heat-metered communal, no open-flued appliances in bedrooms/bathrooms), then boilers/controls/pipework insulation, heat pumps/oil/electric, UFH + unvented technical detail (regulatory G3 content moved to the Water note), and the corrected lighting paragraph. 12. **Regulation 16 ×2 and CDM ×2 (General Notes).** First paragraph of "Statutory Notices, CDM and HMO" (notice periods and inspection stages) dropped; the Regulation 16 detail note moved to the head of General Notes with its inspection-stage list rewritten for a conversion (separating walls/floors and fire stopping before closing up, completion certificate for each flat). Note rewritten accordingly, with a new paragraph explaining that renovated floors/walls must still meet C2 in full as building work under Regulation 4. The "does not apply" list is unchanged (O1, S1, M4, H3, A1–A3 all confirmed not listed); duplicated Part O consultation sentence in its NOTE removed. Site Preparation note now says "C1(2) and C2(c) apply".

Rainwater goods sentence repeated in External Works removed.
17. **Linings note** B4(2) sentence now cross-refers to the External Fire Spread note instead of restating it.
18. **Build-ups.** "Existing External Wall — Internally Insulated": header said `u:"0.30"` while the text said 72.5 mm calculates at 0.26; the first paragraph named no product or thickness. ## (iii) Noticed but deliberately left

- Common-stair rebuild figures (pitch ≤38°, rise 150–170, going ≥250, width ≥1000 mm): not in FACTS; the 38° figure comes from BS 5395-1 rather than AD K, but the clause is conservative and not wrong, so left as written.
- Emergency lighting trigger ("floor more than 4.5 m above ground or stair has no external window") — AD B Vol 1 Table 3.3 could not be retrieved from the fetched PDF; the wording is more onerous than the AD, not less, so left.
- Glazing in a protected entrance hall "fire-resisting to 1.1 m" — Table B5 not retrievable; wording left.
- Roof insulation at ceiling level build-up gives no thickness (u 0.16). Adding a depth would be inventing a figure; left for the calculator check.
- Downlighter prohibition appears in the platform-floor build-up and the heating/lighting note; both are appropriate places, left.
- "Extract ducts passing through unheated voids to be insulated" appears in the Condensation note as well as the Ventilation note — one clause, two relevant contexts, left.
- Regulation 38 appears once (Consultations, fire and rescue authority paragraph) — present as the tests require; not duplicated into the fire section.
- The G2 note's 125 l/p/d: FACTS gives it for new dwellings; Regulation 36(1) applies the same figure to a dwelling formed by material change of use, so stated.

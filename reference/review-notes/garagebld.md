## (i) Verdict

Would building control approve? **Yes, on the substance** — the technical content was already sound and the type-specific items the brief asked for are present and correct: Schedule 2 Class 6 limits (≤30 m², no sleeping accommodation, ≥1 m from the boundary or substantially non-combustible; ≤15 m² regardless; Class 7 carport ≤30 m² open on two sides; floor area measured internally; Part P still applies to an exempt building supplied from the house), attached-garage separation (REI 30, FD30S doorset with self-closer, 100 mm step or fall, roof-void separation), AD A Section 2C small-building wall rules, BROOF(t4) within 6 m, the Section 11 unprotected-area table, Reg 16 notices, AD S 7 kW charge point, Part L applicability and the 2026-edition transition flag.

What a plan-checker would have marked up was presentation: the same subject stated two or three times in different words (foundations three times, DPC/cavity trays/movement joints twice, boundary fire spread twice, Reg 16 notices twice, room-over-garage twice, vehicle-door lintel twice, garage separation restated in the note that sits next to the build-up), one internal contradiction (movement-joint spacing for blockwork), one garbled AD A rule, and two places where the practice's house standards for alarms and escape windows were not fully stated.

## (ii) Findings and fixes

1. **Foundations stated three times → one note.** "Raft and Stepped Foundations", "Foundations" and the shared "Foundations — Fallback Dimensions and Rules" repeated the 750/1000 mm depths, the stepping rule (three times, with two different overlap rules), the attached-to-house depth rule (twice), NHBC 4.2 trees (twice) and rafts (twice). Merged into "Foundations — Strip, Trench Fill, Raft and Steps": paragraph 1 dimensions and AD A 2E rules, paragraph 2 the garage-specific items (depth to match the house foundation with compressible joint, raft as garage floor with door-pier thickening, Party Wall Act s.6 notification, building control inspection where not exempt).
   - Contradiction resolved: step overlap "twice the step or 300 mm" (older note) vs "twice the step, the thickness or 300 mm whichever is greater" (detail note). AD A Diagram 23 is the latter; kept once.
   - Wrong clause fixed: "never less than the projection each side equalling the thickness" was garbled. AD A 2E2 requires the foundation **thickness** to be not less than the projection beyond the wall face and never less than 150 mm — now stated that way (225 mm adopted).
2. **DPC / cavity trays / movement joints stated twice → one note.** Dropped "Damp Proof Courses, Weathering and Junctions"; its unique garage items folded into the detail note (movement joint at the junction with the dwelling, stepped cavity tray in the house wall where the garage roof abuts, DPC linked to the house DPC, copings with DPC and drip, DPC stepped where the ground falls). Retitled "…and Junctions with the Dwelling".
   - Contradiction resolved: blockwork movement joints "6 m" (older note) vs "7.5–9 m for dense concrete blockwork" (detail note). Resolved conservatively to 6 m for concrete blockwork (dense or aircrete), 12 m clay, 7.5–9 m calcium silicate — consistent with the extension fix and the practice's other types.
3. **Boundary / external fire spread stated twice → one note.** Dropped "Walls and Roofs Near the Boundary"; kept the detail note (Section 11 figures 5.6/12/18/24/30 m² verified against FACTS) and folded in the garage-specific paragraph on losing the Class 6 exemption if eaves or gutter come within 1 m ("check against the legal boundary, not the fence"), the polycarbonate-roof caveat and the timber-cladding point. Reworded the latter so the reason is right: combustible cladding counts as unprotected area, which is why a timber-clad wall cannot sit within 1 m — AD B does not itself require non-combustible construction there (that is the Class 6 exemption test). Added the Party Wall notices where built on or astride the boundary.
4. **Regulation 16 stated twice.** The CDM/demolition note opened with its own summary of the notices and inspection stages. Now "CDM 2015, Demolition and Asbestos" points to the Reg 16 note, and the Reg 16 note is tailored to the type: exempt Class 6 garages need no notices (Part P still notified); Reg 46A commencement point for a new detached garage or an attached garage as a horizontal extension (sub-structure incl. ground floor slab complete) — the loft/basement/flat "15 per cent" list, which does not apply to this type, removed; inspection stages and certificates trimmed to garage-relevant ones (Part P, appliance/heat pump/EV charger certificates).
5. **Vehicle-door lintel stated twice.** Removed from "Structural Design" and stated once at the head of "Lintels, Beams and Fire Protection" (150 mm bearing on pier or padstone, deflection to the door manufacturer's limit). "Structural Design" now cross-refers to the Lateral Restraint note for tie-down instead of repeating it.
6. **Separation note restated build-up SW1.** "Attached and Integral Garages" now refers to SW1 in one line (REI 30 / FD30S doorset with self-closer / 100 mm step or fall) and keeps only what SW1 does not say: roof-void separation, not extending the house smoke alarms into the garage (heat alarm to BS 5446-2 optional), 60-minute compartment party wall with notices.
7. **Room over the garage stated twice.** "Partitions, Floor Over and Services" repeated the fire ceiling, exposed-floor insulation and AD K stair from "Home Office or Room Over the Garage". Partitions note now carries the partition and joist specification only and refers across. Home Office note corrected and completed:
   - Escape window now has the full house standard: ≥0.33 m², ≥450 mm each way, bottom of openable area **800–1100 mm** above floor (was upper bound only).
   - Alarm terminology: "interlinked mains smoke alarms to BS 5839-6 Grade D1 Category LD2 … exceeding the Grade D2 Category LD3 minimum" (was "a Grade D1 smoke alarm").
   - Part L volume: the note said the building is "a building other than a dwelling" in every case. Now distinguishes a detached garage (AD L Volume 2, BRUKL or agreed elemental approach) from an attached garage (an extension — AD L Volume 1 Table 4.2), and states that the 0.18/0.15/0.18/1.4 figures are the practice's adopted dwelling new-element values (they are Vol 1 Table 4.2 values, not Vol 2 values).
8. **Lighting efficacy misplaced.** The 75 lumens per circuit-watt requirement appeared only inside the partitions note. Moved to "Electrical Installation and EV Charging" (Services) with the AD L Vol 1 Section 6 reference, applicability (attached or heated garage; adopted as practice standard otherwise) and external-lighting daylight/PIR control per FACTS.
9. **Terminology.** "a FD30S fire door" → "FD30S doorset with self-closer"; the plain "Grade D1 smoke alarm" → full BS 5839-6 grade/category wording; "Approved Document K/L/B Volume n" retained throughout; "building control" lower case as house style.

Figures checked against FACTS and found correct (left as is): wall ties 900 × 450 / 300 mm within 225 mm of jambs; DPC 150 mm; cavity fill 225 mm below DPC; unprotected areas 1 m² / 4 m apart and the Section 11 table; BROOF(t4) within 6 m; escape window 0.33 m² / 450 mm / 4.5 m; AD K guarding 900/1100, 2 m headroom; Reg 16 2 clear days / 5 days / completion-notice contents; AD L Table 4.2 0.18/0.15/0.18/1.4; 2026 editions in force 24 March 2027, transition to 24 March 2028; AD S 7 kW Type 2; AD M socket heights 450–1200 mm.

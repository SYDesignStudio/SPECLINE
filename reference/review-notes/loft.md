# Loft Conversion (`loft`) — plan-check review and fix

Data file reviewed and edited: `/tmp/review/specdata_core.LOFT.js` (loft segment only; extension and flat segments verified byte-identical before/after).
Fix script: `/tmp/review/fix_loft_review.py` (run once; idempotency not intended — re-running will fail on the first `note_block` lookup, by design).

## (i) Verdict

As originally written: **conditional — building control would raise queries before approval.** The fire strategy was internally inconsistent (protected stair enclosure stated as REI 30 in one place and EI in another; doors given as "E 20", "FD20" and "E 30" in three places with a note telling the reader not to use FD references at all), the staircase guarding figure was wrong (1100 mm to landings), the Part G hot water clause was wrong (storage "limited to 60°C", TMV cited to G5), the commencement-notice trigger was wrong for a loft (structural start instead of 15 % complete), and a paragraph about the fire resistance of the new floor had been pasted into the Planning note. Several subjects were printed twice (stairs, ventilation, alarms/rented alarms, heating/hot water, planning/PD, statutory notices, drainage).

As now edited: **approvable.** One consistent escape strategy (protected stairway, Options 2/3 as agreed alternatives), one door standard (FD30S doorset with self-closer exceeding the E 20 minimum; existing doors may be upgraded to E 20), one alarm standard (Grade D1 LD2 exceeding D2 LD3), REI/EI/E notation used correctly, Part G, Part K, Part L (Tables 4.2/4.3) and Reg 16 figures corrected to FACTS, and every subject stated once.

## (ii) Findings and fixes

1. **Planning — duplicate + misplaced paragraph.** "Planning Status and Permitted Development" and "Permitted Development Limits (Class B)" both described Classes B/C. Merged into one note keeping the detailed limits (40/50 m³, ridge, principal elevation, 200 mm eaves set-back, obscure glazing below 1.7 m, Class C 150 mm) plus the scope statements (planning status to be confirmed; BR approval ≠ planning; Article 4 / designated land / listed). The fifth paragraph of the Planning note — about BRE Digest 208 upgrading of a retained ceiling to give the new floor 30 minutes — was a paste error; moved to a new **Loft Floor** note "Fire Resistance of the New Floor over a Retained Ceiling" (that category had a build-up but no note) and its "18 mm deck" reconciled with the build-up's 22 mm ("not less than 18 mm; 22 mm specified").
2. **Statutory notices — duplicate with a wrong clause.** "Statutory Notices and Inspections" said work is "treated as commenced when the structural works are begun"; for a loft the Reg 46A trigger is 15 % complete (FACTS). Dropped and merged into the Reg 16 note; the Reg 16 note's generic inspection list (excavations, foundations, oversite) replaced with loft stages (steel before casing, floor/stair enclosure before closing, roof insulation and VCL before boarding, drains, completion). Completion-certificate/solicitor sentence now stated once. Added the FHS transition flag (AD L1/F1 2026 editions, in force 24 March 2027 — FACTS) as a designer prompt.
3. **Staircases — duplicate with a wrong figure.** "Staircase to the Loft" and "Staircases — Detail" both gave the AD K stair rules. The loft note said "guarding … 900 mm to stairs and 1100 mm to landings" — wrong; AD K Diagram 3.1 is 900 mm for stairs, landings and internal floor edges, 1100 mm for external balconies (verified on the official PDF, see (v)). Merged into one note keeping the detail content plus the loft-specific items (Diagram 1.4 reduced headroom, alternating-tread limits, stairwell guarding before use). The existing designer NOTE that the 1.9 m / 1.8 m values sit in Diagram 1.4 and should be confirmed is accurate (confirmed the figures are in the diagram, not the text) and retained.
4. **Fire strategy — three overlapping notes, inconsistent notation.** "Protected Stairway", "Means of Escape from the New Storey" and "Escape Options for a Two-Storey House Becoming Three" merged into "Escape Strategy — Protected Stairway and the Alternatives": one statement that escape windows are not acceptable above 4.5 m, the three AD B options, the bungalow-to-two-storey window case, inner rooms, and the existing lower-storey route. Escape window now carries the full envelope: ≥0.33 m², ≥450 × 450, bottom of openable area ≤1100 mm and ≥800 mm unless guarded, no key (was "cill not more than 1100 mm" only in one note).
5. **Fire doors — contradiction.** "Fire Doors" said E 20 minimum; "Escape Options" said "FD20 fire doors"; the garage-door note said "E 30 standard with cold smoke seals"; "Fire Stopping and Cavity Barriers" told the reader to "specify by the Euroclass notation rather than the old FD references", which contradicts the firm's FD30S house wording. Resolved: new doors onto the protected stair are **FD30S doorsets (E 30 Sa, intumescent strips and cold smoke seals) with self-closers**, stated as exceeding the AD B Appendix C minimum of E 20 (FD20) without self-closer; existing solid doors may be upgraded to the E 20 minimum with building control's agreement; hollow-core doors replaced with FD30S. Garage door reworded to FD30S doorset. The notation paragraph moved into "Minimum Periods of Fire Resistance" and rewritten to explain REI 30 / EI 30 / E 30 Sa (FD30S) / E 20 (FD20) rather than prohibit FD references.
6. **Stair enclosure REI vs EI.** The Fire Resisting Partition build-up and the Protected Stairway note said REI 30; the Minimum Periods note said EI for the stair enclosure. A non-loadbearing stud enclosure is EI 30. Build-up and notes now say "30 minutes fire resistance (EI 30, or REI 30 where loadbearing)". Materials unchanged.
7. **Alarms — duplicate.** "Fire Detection and Alarm" (D1 LD2, correct) and "Smoke Alarms in Rented Dwellings" (D1 LD2 again plus the 2015/2022 landlord regulations) merged into one note: firm standard D1 LD2 stated once as exceeding AD B's D2 LD3, siting rules, CO alarm, the landlord regulations as a third paragraph, HMO cross-referred to the HMO note (was repeated), certificate once.
8. **Fire stopping / cavity barriers.** Kept as one paragraph; added the eaves and roof/party-wall junction barriers and cross-reference to the party wall build-up; the "no FD references" sentence removed (see 5).
9. **Windows — repeated figure.** "Windows and Glazing" repeated the rooflight 2.2 / 1.6 figure from "Rooflights". Removed; windows now cite Table 4.2 (1.4 or WER B; 1.2 specified) and refer to the Rooflights note; added competent-person (FENSA/CERTASS) notification for replacement windows.
10. **Ventilation ×2 → ×1.** "Background and Extract Ventilation" (had the intermittent rates but "equivalent areas given in AD F" undefined) and "Ventilation — AD F Figures, Systems and Commissioning" (had the areas but not the intermittent rates) merged. All FACTS figures present once: 8000/10,000/4000 mm² at ≥1700 mm; purge 1/20 and 1/10; 10 mm undercut; extract 15/6/30 and kitchen 30 hood / 60; continuous 13/8/6 and 0.3 l/s/m²; hood 650–750 mm; commissioning results to building control within 5 days. Loft-specific clauses kept: do not worsen existing provision, trickle vents in rooflight heads ordered with the units, ducts insulated through eaves voids and never discharging into the roof or eaves voids.
11. **Drainage ×3 → ×2.** The shared "Drainage — Depths, Chambers, Branch Pipes, Building Over Sewers and Pumped Drainage" note (largely below-ground detail for a groundworks job) overlapped "Foul Drainage" (75 mm traps and BS EN 12380 AAV in both). Merged into "Foul Drainage": branch lengths (1.7/3/3/4/6 m), 200 mm rule, long-radius bend, macerator only where a gravity WC exists (very relevant to loft en-suites), stack termination and AAV once, fire stopping/intumescent collar, and a single paragraph for a new below-ground connection (600/900 cover, bedding, rocker pipes, chamber, test before backfilling in the BCO's presence, build-over agreement). Chamber sizing, pumped stations and the slab-mesh detail dropped as not relevant to a loft.
12. **Heating / hot water ×2 → ×1 with a wrong Part G clause.** "Hot Water and Heating" said "Water discharged from the hot water storage system to be limited to 60°C by an in-line tempering valve in accordance with AD G3" and cited the bath TMV to "AD G5". Both wrong: G3 requires the cylinder to be capable of storage at ≥60°C and the bath supply limited to ≤48°C by a TMV to BS EN 1111/1287 (G5 is sanitary conveniences). Dropped and merged into "Heating Systems…": loft-specific items folded in (system capacity for the added load, TRVs on new radiators, relocated/removed loft cistern or F&E tank, pipework in eaves voids insulated to Table 4.4); G3 stated correctly once; 48 °C stated once (asserted in the script); the "commissioning certificates" sentence from the Conservation note folded in here so it is said once.
13. **Conservation of Fuel & Power — deferred standards replaced with figures.** "Existing Thermal Elements and Fixed Building Services" said "upgraded to the standard for a renovated thermal element" without the figures; now states Table 4.3 (roof 0.35 → 0.16, cavity wall 0.70 → 0.55, other walls 0.70 → 0.30, floor 0.70 → 0.25) and the 0.30 target for a retained gable/party wall entering the envelope (consistent with the two upgrade build-ups), plus the technical/functional/economic (15-year payback) feasibility route. "Upgrading the Existing Roof Covering" likewise now gives 0.35 → 0.16 and notes the room-in-roof build-ups (0.14/0.15) satisfy both Table 4.2 and 4.3. "Thermal Elements and Thermal Bridging" now cites Table 4.2 by name and adds doors 1.4.
14. **Structure — repeated clause.** 150 mm bearing / padstone clause was in both "Structural Design — General" and "Steelwork and Fire Protection"; the steelwork note now refers to the structural note.
15. **Dormer Construction note** repeated the leadwork detail and the boundary check that the Leadwork and External Fire Spread notes give with figures; rewritten to cross-refer and to carry the framing/tying-back statement instead.
16. **Chimneys (Part J)** was purely a new-appliance note; added the loft-specific clause on existing chimneys passing through the new storey (40 mm from chimney outer face / 200 mm from flue inner face — the AD J figures already in the note — flue kept in use or capped and ventilated, chimney breast removal to engineer's design).
17. **Build-up wording (materials unchanged):**
    - Hip to Gable masonry gable said "100 mm cavity fully filled with 90 mm Kingspan Kooltherm K106" — self-contradictory (a 90 mm board is partial fill). Reworded to "100 mm cavity with 90 mm Kooltherm K106 Cavity Board (0.019 W/mK) retained against the inner leaf" and a designer NOTE added: a 10 mm residual cavity is less than a partial-fill certificate normally allows — confirm the cavity width and the BBA residual-cavity limit, or use the timber-frame gable.
    - Tile-hung Dormer Cheek gave no product or achieved value while the rendered and boarded cheeks (identical framing) stated 140 mm K112 at 0.18; the tile-hung cheek now states the same product and calculated value "as the rendered cheek".
    - Fire Resisting Partition: REI 30 → "30 minutes (EI 30, or REI 30 where loadbearing)" (see 6).

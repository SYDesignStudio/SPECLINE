## (i) Verdict

Would building control approve the specification as now printed? Yes, as a full-plans specification for a block with no storey more than 11 m above ground (which is the stated basis). The 14 build-ups are internally consistent and the notes now read once per subject, category by category, with the fire figures consistent with Approved Document B Volume 1 and the Part E / L / F / G / O figures consistent with FACTS.md. Before the fix a plan-checker would have queried three things: the stair-head smoke vent area (1.5 m² given where AD B asks 1 m²), the Building Height note placing evacuation alert systems at 11 m while the Fire Detection note placed them at 18 m, and a car-park ventilation reference to the wrong Approved Document (B Vol 1 Section 11, which is boundary distances) — plus the same repeat-notes problem as the extension (stairs ×2, heating ×2, hot water ×3, ventilation ×2, drainage ×3, Part O ×2, escape-in-flat ×2, Reg 16 ×2, Part E testing ×2). A block over 11 m or 18 m still needs the clause-by-clause review the Height note calls for; that is a genuine designer prompt and has been kept.

## (iii) Noticed but deliberately left

- **Common stair "pitch not more than 38 degrees"**: not an AD K figure (AD K gives rise 150–170 / going ≥250 for general-access stairs, which caps pitch at ~34°); 38° is the BS 5395-1 semi-public limit. Redundant but not wrong; left. Likewise the "further rail at 600 mm where children use the stair" (an AD K schools provision) — left, harmless.
- **Dead-end corridor "longer than 7.5 m to be subdivided"**: could not verify the subdivision length from the AD B diagrams via the fetch; left unchanged rather than invent.
- **Cavity barriers "at not more than 20 m centres in ceiling voids"**: AD B Table 9.1 gives 20 m only for Class A1/A2 cavity surfaces (10 m otherwise); not verified in this pass, so left.
- **Fire service vehicle access wording** (45 m of every point on the ground floor / 45 m of every flat entrance door where >11 m and no fire main): AD B Vol 1 para 13.x wording differs slightly (45 m of every dwelling entrance door or 18 m of a fire main inlet); not fetched, left.
- **Background ventilator "four in a one-bedroom / five otherwise"**: shared AD F text already used on the extension; not in FACTS, left.
- **Build-up 13 (pitched roof)**: claims 0.11 W/m²K "allowing for the joist fraction at 400 mm centres" while describing trussed rafters (usually 600 mm centres). Not self-contradictory on the U-value (400 mm of 0.044 mineral wool bridged by timber calculates at about 0.11) so left; the calculator owner may wish to state the tie spacing.
- **Build-up 12 (warm deck)**: roof insulation "Class A2-s1,d0 within 1.5 m of a boundary or higher wall where >11 m" is a designer's conservative rule rather than an explicit AD B clause; left as stated.
- **Separating floor "typically 365 kg/m²"**: mass depends on the chosen Robust Detail; not a checked figure in FACTS, left.
- **Lifts note**: M4(2)/M4(3) lift trigger wording left; the AD M lift provisions are conditional and the note already defers to the planning condition.
- **FHS transition NOTE appears twice** (Services heating note — no fossil boiler; Part L note — edition/transition dates). Both are genuine designer prompts about different consequences and agree on the dates (in force 24 March 2027, transition to 24 March 2028); kept.
- Build-up materials/thicknesses untouched (checked by calculator).

## Resolved 6 September 2026 — car park ventilation, confirmed and corrected

Approved Document F Volume 2 was downloaded and paragraphs 1.38 to 1.40 read directly. The library's
figure was **half** the required opening area, and the rest of the sentence was misattributed.

| Claim in the library | What AD F Volume 2 says |
|---|---|
| Openings of not less than 2.5 per cent of the floor area | Para 1.39a: minimum aggregate equivalent area of **1/20** of the floor area **at each parking level** — 5 per cent. |
| Half at each of two opposite sides | Para 1.39a: not less than **25 per cent** of the aggregate equivalent area on each of two opposing walls. |
| Six air changes per hour for pollution control | Para 1.39b: **3 ach** with permanent openings of 1/40 of the floor area, **or 6 ach for a basement car park**. Six is right only for the basement case. |
| Ten air changes per hour for smoke clearance | Para 1.39b: ten is the **local** rate at **exits and ramps where cars queue**. It is pollution control, not smoke clearance. |
| Fans rated 300 °C for 60 minutes, secondary supply | Not in AD F. Para 1.38's NOTE sends fire ventilation to Approved Document B. |
| (absent) | Para 1.38, the **primary** route: 30 ppm carbon monoxide averaged over 8 hours, 90 ppm peak over 15 minutes. The library omitted it entirely. |

Also corrected: item 9 above changed the reference to "AD B Volume 2 Section 18 (car parks)". Note 7
to Table B2 of AD B Volume 1 gives it as **Section 11** of Volume 2. That volume is not held in this
library, so the smoke clearance provisions, the fan rating and the secondary supply are now a NOTE
on the entry telling the designer to obtain them, rather than figures stated on our authority.
Figures recorded in `reference/FACTS.md` under AD F Volume 2.

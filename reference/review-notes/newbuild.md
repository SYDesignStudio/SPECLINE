## (i) Verdict

As printed before the fix, a plan-checker would have approved the technical content but sent it back for tidying: the same "shared detail" append problem as the extension — ventilation twice, staircases twice, foundations twice, drainage three times over, heating/hot water twice, Reg 16 notices twice, radon twice, movement joints/cavity trays/DPCs in three notes, chimneys/CO twice — plus three real errors (FD20 doors to a three-storey protected stair, a 60/30-minute contradiction on garage separation within the same note, and a 2.75 m gateway width for fire appliance access) and one Part L wording error (rooflight U-value "assessed in the vertical plane"). After the fix the specification reads once per subject, the figures agree with FACTS.md, and I would expect building control to approve it without technical queries. Build-ups were read for internal consistency and left unchanged.

Detail note moved up beside Site Preparation, retitled "Radon and Ground Gas Protection", and given a genuine designer prompt (NOTE — confirm the radon status for the postcode before the floor build-up is issued).
3. **Movement joints ×3, wall ties ×2, lintels ×2, lateral restraint ×2** — "Structural Design — General", "Lintels and Movement", the appended "Movement Joints, Wall Ties, Cavity Trays and Damp Proof Courses" and "Beams, Lintels and Fire Protection" overlapped. **Contradictions resolved**

18. **Garage separation 60 vs 30 minutes** — the old fire-resistance note said "60 minutes to the separating wall *and to any wall or floor separating an integral garage*" in paragraph 1 and "30 minutes fire-resisting construction" for the garage in paragraph 2. Resolved to REI 30 (FACTS; AD B Vol 1 garage separation), 60 minutes retained for the compartment (separating) wall and for a house of four or more storeys.
19. **Window sill 800 mm (AD K) vs 1100 mm (AD O)** — three notes quoted one or other figure without saying why they differ. Guarding note now states the AD K 800 mm guarding rule and points to the Part O rule for openings relied on for night cooling; the Part O note states the 1100 mm / Table 3.1 rule.

**Wrong or weak clauses fixed**

20. **FD20 doors to a three-storey protected stairway** → "FD30S doorsets (E 30 Sa, intumescent strips and cold smoke seals) fitted with self-closers", noting that AD B does not require self-closers in a dwellinghouse and they are the house standard (FACTS: "FD30 doors, FD30S adopted"). The FD20/E 20 door no longer exists in AD B Vol 1 (2019 edition).
21. **Fire appliance access "2.75m at gateways"** → **3.1 m** (AD B Vol 1 vehicle access table, as reproduced by Cornwall Fire & Rescue — see sources). 3.7 m between kerbs, 45 m hose distance, 20 m dead-end turning and 12.5 t were correct and kept.
22. **Rooflight U-value "assessed in the vertical plane as the manufacturer declares"** → "assessed in the horizontal position as AD L Vol 1 Table 1.1 requires (SAP 10 Appendix R corrects for the installed pitch) and on the developed area including any kerb supplied with it" (AD L Vol 1 2021 Table 1.1 note and Table 4.1 notes — fetched and quoted).
23. **Table 4.1 limiting values incomplete** — "Energy Performance Targets" listed walls/floors/roofs/windows-doors/AP only; party wall 0.20 and rooflights 2.2 W/m²K added (FACTS / AD L Table 4.1).
24. **Part O table numbering** — the old note said glazing *and* free areas were "Tables 1.1 and 1.2"; in AD O, Tables 1.1–1.2 are the glazing limits and Tables 1.3–1.4 the minimum free areas. Corrected. The quoted example percentages ("18 per cent … 6 per cent in bedrooms and 4 per cent in other rooms") did not match any single row of the tables as fetched (bedroom minimum is 4 % moderate / 13 % high; the whole-dwelling figure is "the greater of x % of floor area or y % of glazing area") and were removed rather than replaced, because the two fetches of the AD O PDF returned rows in an order I could not reconcile with confidence. The shading options (g ≤ 0.4 with LT ≥ 0.7, external shutters, 50° overhang on due-south facades — AD O para 1.9) were confirmed verbatim and kept.
25. **House terminology** — "a FD30S self-closing door" (two places) → "an FD30S doorset (E 30 Sa, intumescent strips and cold smoke seals) fitted with a self-closer"; "degrees Celsius" → °C in the merged services/G notes; every fire door reference now uses the doorset wording.
26. **AD J combustion air on an airtight new build** — added to the chimney paragraph that the 550 mm²/kW allowance applies to the whole rated output (no 5 kW deduction) where the design air permeability is below 5 m³/(h·m²), which is the normal case for a new dwelling (AD J 2010 Table 2.1 / para 2.2 — from knowledge of the document, not FACTS; flagged here for the reviewer).
